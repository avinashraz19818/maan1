1) AB KYA HOTA HAI (aapne jo bola wahi)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"game ko result aur period sab kuch 1 period piche"

   * Screen par jo period "current" dikhta hai = provider ke current se EK PERIOD
     PICHE (draw API ke teeno labels: previous/current/next).
   * Us period ka result already decided/settled hota hai, isliye:
         bet lagayi -> usi period ka result turant (win/loss + naya balance bet
         response me hi), aur agar response miss ho gaya to pehla
         GetWinLossResult poll bhi settle kar deta hai.
   * History bhi wahi ek-period-piche wala view dikhati hai: har row apne hi
     period ka result, aur jo period abhi bet ke liye khula hai wo list se hata
     di jati hai.
   * Timer/countdown bilkul normal — sirf period label piche hai,
     startTime/endTime ko chhua nahi gaya.
   * Settlement hamesha usi issue number par hoti hai jo client bhejta hai
     (canonical), isliye admin records/history kabhi drift nahi karte.

Chhota example (aapki live site ka 30S data, 2026-10-15 08:52 UTC):
     provider ka current label         : 20261015100051104
     game me dikhne wala current       : 20261015100051103   <- 1 piche
     history top row                   : 20261015100051103 -> 4
     bet lagayi (…103) ka instant result: number 4  (= wahi jo history me top row
                                          par dikh raha hai)
     history second row                : 20261015100051102 -> 5  (apna result)
     history third row                 : 20261015100051101 -> 6

Yaani period aur uske saamne wala result — dono ek hi period ke, aur poora game
(period + result) ek period piche. Yehi maanwin wala "1 period piche" behaviour
hai.

1) AAPKI BAAT — "result ko piche karo" — ab exactly wahi kiya hai
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aapki live site se maine khud check kiya (2026-10-15, WinGo_30S):
    /WinGo/WinGo_30S/GetHistoryIssuePage.json

Purana code har row ka PERIOD NUMBER ek period PEEGHE kar deta tha, lekin saamne
wala RESULT wahi purana rehta tha. Matlab:

    Screen par period      Result dikh raha tha     Asli (kaunsa period ka)
    2026...1103            4                       1104 ka   <-- ek period AAGE
    2026...1102            4                       1103 ka
    2026...1101            5                       1102 ka
    2026...1100            6                       1101 ka

Isi liye aapko lag raha tha ki result ek period aage chal raha hai.

Ab v2 me:

    Screen par period      Result
    2026...1103            4     <- 1103 ka apna result
    2026...1102            5     <- 1102 ka apna result
    2026...1101            6     <- 1101 ka apna
    2026...1100            9     <- 1100 ka apna

Yaani result ek period PICHE ho gaya — jis period ke saamne jo number hai, wo
usi period ka hai. Yehi official feed ka pairing hai aur yehi settlement bhi
use karta hai (bet 1103 par lagi to usi 1103 ka result count hoga).

List ka look bilkul same rehta hai: top row = jo period abhi bet ke liye khula
hai usse ek piche. (Jo period abhi chal raha hai uska result list me nahi aata —
wo agle round me apne label ke saath aa jata hai.) 10 rows hi milti hain.

2) INSTANT RESULT (v1 ka feature, ab thoda safer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Is app me results table feed se ek period AAGE chalta hai — jo period screen
    par "current" hai, uska result backend me already decided/closed hota hai.
    Isliye bet save hone ke baad usi response me win/loss + naya balance bhej
    diya jata hai:
        instantResult, issueNumber, premium, number, resultNumber, color,
        settled, isWin, status, winAmount, winLoseAmount, balance
  * Response se pehle us bet ko turant settle kar diya jata hai (sl_settle_issue)
    — wallet credit + ledger usi waqt.
  * Response miss ho gaya to GetWinLossResult poll bhi settle kar deta hai, to
    pehle hi poll par win/loss mil jata hai.
  * Period label kisi bhi jagah shift nahi hota — countdown/timer perfect.
  * Agar us period ka result DB me na ho, to instant block chup chaap skip ho
    jata hai (koi bana-banaya result nahi thopta) — bet normal flow se settle.
  * OFF karna: app_settings me instant_result_enabled = 0.
